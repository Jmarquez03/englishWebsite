The Review

Highlights

Quick hits for players who skim like speedrunners.

Deep Inquiry

Successfully merging primary survey data from the South Texas region with secondary academic scrolls created an evidence base for the $188,200 wealth gap.

Vocal Adaptation

Overcoming the Robotic Tone penalty in the Podcast Tavern level by reskilling into a casual, conversational speech style was a satisfying journey that allowed me to effectively engage the community.

The UI Overhaul

The "Green for Growth" color schemes and hierarchical layout of the game's HUD to direct user attention to the critical socioeconomic disparity.

Multiplayer Synergy

Playing the game with friends allowed me to see different styles of playing and apply them to my solo playthrough.

Verdict

ENGL 1302: The Quest for Advocacy is a masterpiece of a research based RPG. It is a must-play for any student of the course.
